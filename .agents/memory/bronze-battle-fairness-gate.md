name: Bronze battle fairness gate
description: Keeps bot battles as stored verse exchanges until dialect-fairness validation is complete.
---

Bronze-tier bot battles must record the player's verse and Beef's response without assigning scores, damage, judgments, or winners.

**Why:** Automated battle judging is explicitly gated behind a separate dialect-fairness validity audit, including AAVE bias testing.

**How to apply:** Future battle tiers may extend the session model and response system, but must not enable automated comparative evaluation until the audit is confirmed complete.
